Teaching Methodology

Concept → Reasoning → Speed → Accuracy

A structured approach designed for students targeting competitive GMAT scores.

01

Diagnose

Identify mathematical strengths, weaknesses and recurring error patterns.

02

Build Fundamentals

Strengthen arithmetic, algebra, geometry, statistics and quantitative reasoning fundamentals.

03

Develop Strategies

Learn multiple approaches and identify the most efficient method for different question types.

04

Practice

Solve progressively challenging quantitative and data reasoning problems.

05

06

Improve Speed & Accuracy
