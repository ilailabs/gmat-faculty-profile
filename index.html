Engineering Mathematics with an Engineering Mindset

Elankovan Gopal is an IIT Madras alumnus and engineering educator with extensive experience teaching mathematics from undergraduate engineering level to competitive examinations.


His teaching approach focuses on developing conceptual clarity, mathematical intuition and problem-solving ability rather than relying only on formula memorization.


For GATE aspirants, the focus is on understanding the underlying concepts, identifying recurring problem patterns, solving numerical problems efficiently and developing exam-oriented problem-solving strategies.
